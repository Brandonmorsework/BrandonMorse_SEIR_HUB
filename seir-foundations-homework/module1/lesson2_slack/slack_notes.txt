hw4: 
Why is a quiet INFO here GOOD design, not a failure?

Similarly to script 2 that was ran, quiet info is a good design because alerts channels should be used for updating users on important info or edge-cases that require their attention. 
If users were updated for the smallest changes in disk usage, they would learn to ignore it, training them to become blind to important warnings that could cause instance failure.
